How the week sits

The house plan is San Pedro, on Ambergris Caye. That is the dock the Blue Hole boats leave from, and it is where the Christmas boat parade happens. Caye Caulker is one water taxi away, about half an hour, and that is where the spearfishing account lives. If the rental is already on Caye Caulker, tell Reid and the two boat days trade places.

Belize does not change the clocks. In December the time on the island matches Chicago.

Nothing on this page is reserved yet. The prices are what the operators had published when this was put together on November 23, 2026. December fills, and a rough sea moves a boat day. Book the three water days this week, and ask each one for a weather rebook.

Monday · everyone

The Blue Hole

One long day. Leave around 6am, back between 4:30 and 5:30. Three stops: the Hole, Half Moon Caye for lunch, and a reef called the Aquarium. Plan it as snorkeling unless someone has a recent dive to 80 feet.

$280–$305 a person on the shared boat, all in

That shared boat still needs about 10 people to leave. A Monday is the thinner crowd. The private boat is the one that is only us. Belize Pro Dive lists it around $5,000 to $6,000 for the boat before tax, and their page disagrees on whether that covers four people or more. For five, get a yes in writing. Split five ways, that is about $1,200 a person before tax and a $40 cash park fee.

Before the boats

License, cart, parade

Anyone fishing needs a sport fishing license from the coastal authority. The published one-day fee is BZ$20. Ask each captain if it is already in the boat price. The Shack’s listing says it is included on their boat.

If the house is outside the middle of town, rent a 6-seater golf cart. A foreign license is fine. From December 20, J and H’s seasonal day rate was $100 for a 6-seater before gas. A week needs their quote.

The lighted boat parade has no published 2026 date yet. Last year it was December 20. Hold Sunday evening until the town council posts it.

Friday, December 18

Land, eat like the island, and see who still has legs

All five, then an optional late night

No boat. The first meal should be ordinary and good. El Fogon cooks on a wood fire on a back street, the way a Belizean kitchen does. They are closed Sunday, so Friday is the night.

  1. Afternoon
    Land, get the cart if we need one, and find the house. If anyone is hungry on the way, Neri’s tacos or the Truck Stop. The Truck Stop is a yard of local food trucks, not a restaurant.
  2. 6:30
    Dinner at El Fogon, 2 Trigger Fish Street, just north of the Tropic Air terminal. Call ahead. A group of five picks up a 15% service charge. Open until 9, last seating 8:45. +501 673-2121.
  3. 8:45
    Mom and Dad head back. Friday can be a travel day, and that is a fine place to end it.
  4. 11:00
    If Reid, Chase, and Ben still want a night: Jaguar’s Temple, on Barrier Reef Drive. It is the island’s dance floor, and it is empty if you show up early. It usually runs Thursday through Saturday. Holiday week can change that.

Saturday, December 19

The spear day, on their boat

Parents back in the afternoon

San Pedro Belize Express leaves the San Pedro terminal for Caye Caulker at 7:00am and takes about 30 to 45 minutes. Buy the tickets the day before. December boats fill, and the board at the terminal beats any website if the two disagree. Their schedule is posted here, and it can change.

  1. 6:30
    At the San Pedro water taxi terminal for the 7:00am Express. Tell the Shack we are five, coming from San Pedro, and we want them at the Caye Caulker dock when that boat lands.
  2. Morning
    On their boat. Free-diving only. Mom and Dad can snorkel and ride. Reid, Chase, and Ben spear. The guide picks the patch. It will not be inside a reserve.
  3. 3:45
    The midafternoon Express back to San Pedro. The 3:45pm is a calm one for Mom and Dad. There is also a 5:15. Check the board that morning.
  4. Night
    The night lobster hunt is the Shack’s own trip. If the three stay, ask the guide that morning where three people should sleep. They know a room that is not a party hostel. Parents have a quiet dinner in San Pedro. Caramba is the easy Creole and seafood room.

Sunday, December 20

Keep the evening open for the boats

All five, on the beach

Last year the Christmas Lighted Boat Parade left Central Park beach just after 5:30pm on December 20, ran down the east side of town, and came back. The organizers said it would return in 2026. As of November 10, the island paper still had no date. This year’s December 20 is a Sunday. Stand on that beach with the town. Do not get on a tour boat for it.

  1. 8:45
    If the three slept on Caye Caulker, the regular Express back to San Pedro is 8:45am. The 7:00am Sunday boat is tied to the Mexico run. Take the 8:45.
  2. Day
    Nothing booked. A swim, a cart through the back streets, a late lunch. El Fogon is closed on Sunday.
  3. 4:30
    Eat near Central Park before the boats. The Truck Stop, or a plate from a stall by the park.
  4. 5:30
    On the beach at Central Park, if the council kept the date. If they move it, this evening becomes a normal Sunday and we follow whatever night they post.

Monday, December 21

The Blue Hole, and then an early night

All five · the long day

This is the one famous day. The boat ride is about two and a half hours each way. From the surface, the Hole is a dark circle in the reef. Snorkelers swim the edge. Divers, if anyone is qualified, go deep for a short dive. Then Half Moon Caye, which is a small island with a red-footed booby colony, and lunch. Then the Aquarium, which is the pretty reef of the three.

  1. 5:15
    Pickup at the dock, or a walk to the dive shop. They will say which. Eat something small before. The boat breakfast is light.
  2. 6:00
    Leave San Pedro. Bring the $40 park fee in cash, per person. Hats, reef-safe sunscreen, a light layer. The ride back can be cool and wet.
  3. Midday
    The Hole, then Half Moon Caye. Walk the island. Lunch is included on the usual boats.
  4. 4:30
    Back in San Pedro, give or take an hour. Dinner is whatever is easy and close. Bed. Tuesday is another boat.

A shared seat is about $215 to $235 for a snorkeler before tax, then 12.5% tax, then the $40 cash fee. Call it $280 to $305. Gear rental is extra if we do not bring masks and fins. The private boat is the choice at the top of this page.

Wednesday, December 23

A day that is not a tour

All five for dinner

Ambergris does not have a hike. The quiet version of a nature day is Bacalar Chico, at the north end of the caye. A small boat, a ranger, a short trail, mangroves, and almost nobody. Ask Manny on Tuesday if he will run it Wednesday morning. If the answer is no, the morning is Marco Gonzalez, the small Maya site in the bush south of town. An hour with a local guide. It is not the big ruin bus.

  1. Morning
    Bacalar Chico, or Marco Gonzalez, or nothing. Two boat days sit behind this one. A slow morning is a fair choice.
  2. 2:00
    Lunch in town and a swim. Secret Beach has turned into a row of beach clubs. We are skipping it.
  3. 6:30
    The nicer dinner. Book one table. Blue Bayou was at the top of the San Pedro list in November 2026, with a smaller pile of reviews. Hidden Treasure has about 3,000 reviews and a garden south of town. Either one. This is the dinner if Thursday is a morning flight.
  4. After
    Mom and Dad are done. Wednesday is not a club night. Reid, Chase, and Ben can sit at Wayo’s.

Depends on the flight

Christmas Day is Friday the 25th, after we are gone. A lot of kitchens close on Christmas Eve or switch to a set menu. If the flight is in the morning, Wednesday was the goodbye dinner and Thursday is the airport.

What the captain has to follow

  • Spear on a breath. A tank and a spear is illegal.
  • No spears inside Hol Chan or the other reserves. The guide picks the water.
  • Nassau grouper is closed from December 1 through March 31.
  • Parrotfish and tangs stay in the water. Bonefish, tarpon, and permit are catch and release.
  • Lobster is open. No eggs, no soft shells. A legal tail is at least 4 ounces, and the carapace at least 3 inches.
  • Conch should be open, unless the national quota already closed it. Ask that morning.

Left off on purpose

Secret Beach, the Palapa bar, Shark Ray Alley, cave tubing, and the Altun Ha bus. We have done the tourist loop. The Blue Hole is the one exception, and it is on Monday so the rest of the week can stay small.

There is a cacao fair on December 20. It is in Toledo, in the far south. That is a different trip.

Cash still matters on the island. Park fees, some taxis, and small covers want it. US dollars are used next to Belize dollars. Two Belize dollars is one US dollar.
